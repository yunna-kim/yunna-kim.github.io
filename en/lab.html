Lab

Yunna Kim Lab

The Yunna Kim Lab conducts clinician-led translational research in Korean Medicine neuropsychiatry, connecting clinical questions with clinical practice guidelines, digital biomarkers, neuro-immune mechanisms, and trauma-informed care.

Vision

Research Vision

Clinician-led translational research

Clinical questions from real-world neuropsychiatric care are translated into study designs, clinical data, guidelines, and public education.

Warm science for mental health

The lab integrates biological mechanisms, lifestyle rhythms, psychological trauma, and Korean Medicine therapeutic resources.

Themes

Research Themes

ADHD & Clinical Guidelines

Development of Korean Medicine clinical practice guidelines and clinical pathways for ADHD across the lifespan.

Digital Biomarkers

fNIRS, EEG, head meridian/hemodynamic measures, and prediction of treatment response.

Neuro-immune Axis

LCN2, glial pathology, and neuroinflammation in depression and cognitive disorders.

Trauma-informed Korean Medicine

Disaster response, PTSD, support for survivors of violence, and trauma-informed Korean Medicine care.

Child & Adolescent Mental Health

ADHD, anxiety, sleep, learning issues, tic disorders, and developmental concerns.

Nutritional Psychiatry

Sleep, nutrition, movement, and cortisol/dopamine rhythms as lifestyle foundations for mental health recovery.
